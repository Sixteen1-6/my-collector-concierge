Conditions, in plain English.

The questions collectors ask before they ship. If yours isn't here, your concierge will answer it before anything is charged.

You don't have to grade anything.

Most cards are simply inventoried and sent home or vaulted. You choose, card by card, what is worth a check, a grader, a slab or a sale.

You're charged only after you approve.

Final counts, fees and any credits are confirmed at intake. Estimates never authorize paid work.

Grader fees are passed through at cost.

Our fee covers prep, submission and tracking. The grader's own fee is added as charged. No third-party charge gets a member discount.

Grading bands follow value, not grade.

The fee band is set by the value agreed at intake. There is no extra charge if a card comes back gem mint.

Vault space is counted per card.

One toploader, magnetic holder or slab is one space. Above your plan, extra space is $30 per 200 cards a month, in whole blocks.

Display slabs are display only.

A My CC slab is not professional grading or authentication. Member slab pricing is fixed and doesn't stack with service discounts.

Consignment fees come from the sale.

10% of the item sale price, $10 minimum per sold card, excluding tax and shipping. Marketplace fees pass through separately.

The vault is storage, not insurance.

Vault capacity is not insurance coverage. Coverage is only as stated in the vault agreement.

Estimates are estimates.

An estimate is not a guarantee of card value or grade, and it never authorizes paid work.

Your records stay until you say otherwise.

A card's digital record stays in your inventory until it is sold or you delete it, even after it goes home.
