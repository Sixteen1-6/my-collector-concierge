Before you ship

Do I have to grade anything?

No. Most cards are simply inventoried and sent home or vaulted. You choose, card by card, what is worth a check, a grader, a slab or a sale.

When am I charged?

Final counts, fees and any credits are confirmed at intake. An estimate never authorizes paid work.

Are grader fees marked up?

Grader fees are separate from our fee and appear on your quote. Member discounts apply only to My CC labor, never to grader fees.

Is there a gem-mint fee?

No. There are no gem-mint fees and no submission quotas.

How is vault space counted?

One toploader, magnetic holder or slab is one space. Above your plan it's $30 per 200 cards a month. Vault capacity is not insurance coverage.

Is a display slab a grade?

No. A My CC slab is for display, with no third-party grade assigned.

How does consignment work?

We keep 10% of the item sale price, with a $10 minimum per sold card. Marketplace fees or credits are separate, and your payout and any extra costs are agreed before listing.

What happens to my records?

Every card's record stays in your inventory until it is sold or you delete it, even after it goes home.
